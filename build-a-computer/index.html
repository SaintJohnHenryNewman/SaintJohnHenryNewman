KS3 Computer Science — Inside a Computer

How to play: Fit the motherboard into the case first — once it's in, its real layout appears and you can drag each part onto the socket it belongs to. Try a part before the board's ready and it'll just sit loose in the case until you can see where it really goes. Get the order wrong (like fitting the fan before the CPU) and you'll need to take it back out (drag it to the tray, or hit ✕) and try again. Once each part fits, answer two quick questions to label it with its correct name and definition — get every part in correctly and watch the computer power up!
